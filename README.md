# Pico Kahoot! Answerer
Uses Pushbuttons attached to the Pico to automatically move your cursor and click on Kahoot! answers.

## Supplies
- Raspberry Pi Pico
- 8 jumper wires (M/M)
- 4 Pushbuttons
- MicroUSB cable

## Setup
1. Wire up your buttons to your Pico.
2. Install MicroPython on your Pico.
3. Open Main.py. Change the button Pins to the pins you wired your buttons to.
4. Connect your Pico to your laptop and drag Main.py into the drive. If the drive doesn’t show up, save it to your Pico while it’s connected via Thonny.
5. Open KahootAutoAnswerer.py - replace Serial ID with your Pico’s.
6. Test it out! Make sure your Pico is still connected to your computer.
